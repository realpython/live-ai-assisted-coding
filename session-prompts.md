## Step 1: Aims and Definition of Done

> I'd like to work on a project that's a battle of bots. The way it would work
> is that you'd have a grid, say a 10×10 grid, and you have bots. Each bot is
> defined by a function that gives it its rules, and each bot can do things
> like move up, down, left, or right by one square, attack a bot if it's in a
> neighbouring square, or do nothing, for example. We might come up with some
> other ideas of what we want it to do. Each bot starts with three lives, or
> hit points, or whatever you want to call them, and of course each time it's
> hit, it loses a point. When it gets to zero, it's out. The idea is that
> whoever's left at the end is the winner.

> Could we also have users submit their own bots through a PR? Each bot is
> going to be a function that defines the rules of how the bot behaves.

> I'd also like to be able to visualise this. I don't want any fancy
> visualisation, so text on the terminal screen is good enough, but I'd like
> to be able to visualise a match: seeing the bots move, then disappear as
> they lose, and seeing how many hit points they have. Can we add that to our
> requirements, please?
